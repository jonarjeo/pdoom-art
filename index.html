C Primer

WTF is p(doom), briefly

01

It's a probability. Your probability.

p(doom) = your credence that advanced AI leads to an existential catastrophe — human extinction, or humanity permanently losing control of its future. It's a subjective, Bayesian number: a belief with a percent sign, not a measurement.

02

The spread is the discourse.

Ask researchers, lab CEOs, and the timeline and you'll get anything from <1% to >90%. In a 2023 survey of ~2,700 AI researchers, the median chance given to “extremely bad outcomes (e.g. human extinction)” was about 5%. Nobody thinks that's a small number for extinction.

03

Always ask: doom of what, by when?

Two people saying “20%” can mean totally different things. Extinction or disempowerment? By 2100 or ever? Conditional on what policy? The number is only as good as its definition — which is why “it depends how you operationalize it” is the most p(doom) answer possible.

04

And yes, it's a meme.

“What's your p(doom)?” became the new “what's your sign?” — except the answer can end brunch. The meme is useful: it forces people to put a rough number on a fuzzy fear, then argue about why. This museum collects the art, clips, and chaos around that ritual.
