C Primer

WTF is p(doom), briefly

01

A probability. Yours.

p(doom) = how likely you think advanced AI causes an existential catastrophe — extinction, or humanity permanently losing the steering wheel. Subjective. Bayesian. A belief wearing a % sign, not a lab measurement.

02

The fight is the spread.

Ask researchers, lab CEOs, timeline posters: you'll hear <1% to >90%. In a 2023 survey of ~2,700 AI researchers, the median on "extremely bad outcomes (e.g. human extinction)" was about 5%. For extinction, that's not a shrug.

03

Doom of what, by when?

Two people saying "20%" often aren't saying the same thing. Extinction or disempowerment? By 2100 or ever? Conditional on which policy? The number is only as honest as the definition — which is why "it depends how you operationalize it" is peak p(doom).

04

Also a meme.

"What's your p(doom)?" replaced "what's your sign?" and can nuke brunch. Useful, though: it makes people pick a rough number for a fuzzy dread, then fight about why. This place collects the clips, art, and wreckage from that habit.
